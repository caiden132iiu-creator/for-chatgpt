A weekend in the hills

01

Overview

A gentle two-day route through forest paths and small villages. Start after breakfast and leave time for a long lunch.

What to bring

Comfortable walking shoes, a refillable bottle, a light waterproof layer, and a small notebook for the places you want to revisit.

The plan

Saturday: a riverside walk and a picnic at the mill. Sunday: a morning ridge route, followed by a late lunch in the village.
