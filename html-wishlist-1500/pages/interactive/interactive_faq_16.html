The questions we hear most

01
Can I change my selection later?

Yes. You can update your selection before confirming the request. If you need help choosing, send us a note with the details that matter to you.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

Our team reviews the information and follows up during business hours. Include a preferred date and any access requirements so we can give a useful answer.

Are there additional fees?

The listed price covers the items described. Any optional extras are shown separately before you confirm, so you can review the total first.

Can you accommodate accessibility requests?

Please tell us what would make your visit or experience easier. We can discuss step-free access, quieter times, written information, or other practical arrangements.
