A few helpful answers

02
How intense is the experience?

The after-dark route includes sudden sounds, low light, and theatrical scares. Early dusk sessions reduce the intensity and keep the house lights higher.

Is there an age recommendation?

The full route is recommended for ages 14 and up. Under-16 visitors must attend with an adult. Ask about the gentler daylight family route.

Will performers touch visitors?

No. Performers do not touch guests, and guests must not touch performers or scenery.

Can I leave partway through?

Yes. Tell a steward at any point and they will guide you to the nearest exit. Step-free route details are available before booking.
